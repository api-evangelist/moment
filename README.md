Moment is the AI operating system for investment management and the fixed-income infrastructure that powers bond data, pricing, and trading for digital wealth platforms, RIAs, and brokerages. Through simple REST and WebSocket APIs it delivers institutional-grade reference data on 500,000+ (1M+ global) fixed-income securities — corporates, treasuries, agencies, municipals, CDs, ABS/MBS and structured products — plus real-time and historical evaluated pricing, live top-of-order-book and full order books, and advanced analytics (yield conversion, markups, duration, spreads, liquidity scores, implied default). Its execution management system covers orders, RFQs, smart order routing, allocations, block trades, corporate actions, portfolio optimization, model and asset-allocation management, and account, risk-control, and cash-raise operations. Moment works with wealth firms overseeing more than $10 trillion in assets across 60,000+ advisors.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Moment: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
